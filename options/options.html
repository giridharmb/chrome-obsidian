1 · Connection

Easiest: in the plugin's settings turn on Enable Non-encrypted (HTTP) Server and use http://127.0.0.1:27123 (it only listens on your machine). To use the HTTPS port 27124, first open https://127.0.0.1:27124 and accept the self-signed certificate.
